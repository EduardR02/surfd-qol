HUD concepts

Surfd QoL, round 1 mock-ups

Ten ideas for the in-run HUD. Each one comes as a full version (replays, spectating, or anyone who wants every value) and a surfing version (readable in a glance of about 100 ms, mid-run). Both frames are true scale, cropped from a 1920×1080 screen with the speed at 82 % of the height. The crosshair is at the top centre of each frame.

Every concept shows the same data at the same moment, starting with today's HUD as concept 0. Two mixes close the page. Switch the moment with keys 1 to 4 and cycle the backdrop with B.

Shared rules

  • Ink. Instrument Sans 400/700 from the bundled subset, tabular figures, and today's dark glyph edge with no boxes. Bars and dots use the same edge as a box shadow.
  • Colour. Rose speed, teal for good, gold for close and red for worse. The speed takes the energy tint. Nothing else is coloured unless it is graded.
  • Quiet digits. Fractions of losses and of the timer are set smaller, so “−52” and “00:17” land first.
  • Surfing mode merges down and up into surf, drops the checkpoint's own time and speed, and keeps sync only until the first ramp.
  • Grey means older. Mid-ramp (moment 3), the down/up split and surf belong to the previous ramp, so they turn grey once the new ramp's board arrives.
